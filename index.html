Selected Work

Built one piece at a time.

From dining tables to custom statement pieces, every project is designed and crafted with a focus on quality, character, and lasting function.

01

The Evolve Table

A 10-foot by 4-foot solid cherry conference table with two flip-up power modules.

Solid cherry · Conference table

Our largest table to date, this solid cherry conference table is 10 ft long and 4 ft wide. It features rich grain, a strong architectural base, and two flip-up power modules.

02

The Gathering Table

A solid walnut Dutch-extension dining table. Seats 6–8 closed, up to 14 extended.

Solid walnut · Extension dining table

A solid walnut Dutch extension table designed to bring people together. Seating 6–8 comfortably in its everyday configuration, the integrated extension leaves pull out and rise seamlessly into place, expanding the table to comfortably accommodate up to 14 guests without sacrificing its clean, timeless design.

03

The Woven Console

A Nakashima-inspired wall-mounted walnut console with cloth-backed sliding doors.

Walnut · Wall-mounted console

Nakashima-inspired walnut wall-mounted console featuring cloth-backed sliding doors, warm contrasting accents, and clean, modern lines. Designed to add functional storage while making a statement on the wall.

04

XO Table

A 60-inch solid walnut dining table with a sculpted edge.

Solid walnut · Round dining table

A handcrafted 60-inch solid walnut dining table featuring a beautifully sculpted sloped edge and rich, natural grain. The clean, tapered base and softly rounded profile give this piece a warm, modern character while maintaining a timeless presence.

05

The Rise Table

A white oak and walnut lift-top coffee table.

White oak & walnut · Lift-top coffee table

Lift-top coffee table with a warm white oak top and shelf, contrasted with rich walnut joinery and legs.

06

The Spot Bar

A mobile wedding bar with a maple top and modular flip-up panels.

Maple · Mobile bar

A versatile, handcrafted mobile bar designed to adapt to any wedding setup. Featuring a maple countertop and hinged flip-up panels with integrated extending supports, the bar can be configured as a left L-shape, right L-shape, or a straight bar, giving the venue flexibility to create the perfect layout for every event.

Founder & Maker

Made by hand. Made to matter.

I'm a husband, a father of three, and a furniture maker passionate about creating pieces that are built to last. After ten years as an occupational therapist, I left my career to build furniture full time. Working from my shop in Harrison, Ohio, I use locally sourced hardwoods to handcraft custom heirloom furniture designed to be enjoyed for generations.

“Through Cincy Tables I get to keep chasing that feeling of turning an idea into something real, beautiful, and built to last.”
